# Commercial use

**WhatsApp MCP is free for noncommercial use and needs a separate license for commercial use.**

To ask about a commercial license, write to **Matheus Pina** at <mathpinab@gmail.com>.
Please include who you are, what you want to build or run, and roughly how many users or accounts it will serve.

## What the free license covers

Under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) you may use, modify and share the software for any
noncommercial purpose. That includes:

- personal use, hobby projects, private automation of your own account, and personal study;
- research, experiments and testing for the benefit of public knowledge;
- use by charities, educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations,
  environmental protection organizations and government institutions.

## What needs a commercial license

Anything that is not noncommercial. In practice, contact us before you:

- run it inside a company or for a client, even for internal tooling;
- offer it, or something built on it, as a product or service (SaaS, hosted, white-label, managed);
- embed it in a commercial product or distribute it with one;
- use it to support a revenue-generating activity, such as customer support, sales or marketing automation.

The license text is the authority. If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask before you start.
